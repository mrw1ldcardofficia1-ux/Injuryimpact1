How the score works

Each injury scores 0 to 10 from the player's share of minutes, share of goals and assists, how hard to replace they are, and how tough the next fixtures are. Club scores combine injuries with diminishing returns, so five minor knocks rank below one star player out. Return times are estimates from typical ranges.
